Product Requirements Document

TripMate MVP

A mobile-first shared-trip expense tracker for private groups who need a clear, fair view of contributions, spending, and balances.

Status: DraftScope: MVPCurrency: THBPayments: Manual tracking

1. Product summary

TripMate helps people travelling together record shared-pool contributions and expenses, split costs fairly, and understand who owes what. It replaces chat messages and ad-hoc spreadsheets with a shared, understandable ledger.

The MVP tracks money only. It does not move money between people or connect to payment providers.

2. Problem

Trip companions pay for transport, meals, tickets, and supplies at different times. Groups lose track of who paid, which purchases came from pooled cash, and each person’s ultimate share. Reconciliation at the end of a trip is slow, error-prone, and can cause disagreement.

3. Goals

  • Let an organiser create a private trip and invite people quickly.
  • Give every member a current, understandable view of group funds and spending.
  • Support common expense-sharing cases without manual calculations.
  • Show each traveller an explainable personal bill.
  • Keep an auditable record of contributions and expenses.

4. Non-goals

  • Payment processing, bank/wallet connections, and automated settlement.
  • Public trip discovery or social networking.
  • Multi-currency conversion or exchange rates.
  • Receipt scanning, tax reporting, or accounting integrations.

5. Users

Trip organiser

Creates the trip, shares invitations, manages participants, selects the cash holder, and starts the trip.

Trip member

Joins through an invitation, views the ledger, contributes money, records permitted expenses, and checks their personal bill.

6. Core experience

  1. An organiser creates a trip with their name, trip name, dates, and optional location details.
  2. TripMate creates a private invitation link or code.
  3. Members join with a display name.
  4. The organiser assigns a cash holder and starts the trip.
  5. Members record shared-pool contributions and expenses.
  6. The payer chooses an equal, selected-member, or mixed split.
  7. The ledger updates balances, activity, and total spend.
  8. Each member reviews an itemised personal bill.

7. Functional requirements

FR-1: Create a trip

  • Require organiser name, trip name, and valid start/end dates.
  • Reject an end date before the start date.
  • Make the organiser the first member and open a shareable invitation.

FR-2: Join a trip

  • Join through a valid private invitation link or code.
  • Require a display name and prevent duplicate membership.
  • Show clear errors for invalid, expired, or revoked invitations.

FR-3: Manage the group

  • List joined members and the organiser.
  • Allow the organiser to invite members and assign one cash holder.
  • Allow the organiser to start a trip after at least two members join.

FR-4: Record a contribution

  • Record contributor, THB amount, and optional note.
  • Require a positive amount with at most two decimal places.
  • Update the pool balance and contributor total; add a timestamped activity entry.

FR-5: Record an expense

  • Capture description, payer, payment source, amount, and split method.
  • Support Personal and Shared pool payment sources.
  • Create an immutable ledger entry; future corrections use a reversal/edit flow.

FR-6: Split an expense

  • Everyone: equal split across all active members.
  • Selected people: equal split across chosen members.
  • Mixed: named parts with their own amount and assigned member(s).
  • Show calculations before saving; allocations must exactly equal the expense amount.

FR-7: View the active ledger

  • Show pool balance, recent activity, total used, and actions to add funds or expenses.
  • Identify personal versus shared-pool expenses.
  • Warn before confirming an expense that takes the pool below zero.

FR-8: View a personal bill

  • Show itemised shared allocations, personal extras, paid-into-pool amount, and net due/credit.
  • Every total must be explainable from individual ledger entries.

8. Business rules and calculations

MetricDefinition
Shared pool balanceContributions into the pool minus expenses paid from it.
Member shared-cost totalSum of the member’s allocations from shared expenses.
Member personal extrasExpenses allocated only to that member.
Paid into poolContributions attributed to that member.
Net settlement positionShared costs + personal extras − pool contributions − personally paid expense credits.

Personal-payment credits prevent someone who paid for a shared cost from being charged for it again. The product derives a clear “You owe” or “You are owed” label from this position.

10. UX requirements

  • Mobile-first, following the supplied design.pen screen flow, while providing a convenient responsive web experience on tablets and desktops.
  • Consistent THB formatting and inline validation.
  • Clear success states that return users to updated information.
  • Ledger prioritises visible balance, recent changes, and prominent actions.
  • Personal bills explain calculations without requiring the organiser.
  • Empty states guide groups to add members, funds, or a first expense.

11. Invitations and notifications

The MVP supplies a copyable private invitation link or code. Sending messages, push notifications, email delivery, reminders, and payment requests are deferred. Members see updates when they next open the trip.

12. Authentication and privacy

  • Use lightweight sign-in or persistent device identity to prevent duplicate membership and associate activity.
  • Trips are private; only invited members access their data.
  • Members see only trips they belong to.
  • Store only data necessary for membership, ledger activity, and display names.

13. Success metrics

  • 80% of created trips have two or more members.
  • 60% of started trips record at least one contribution or expense.
  • 50% of active members view their personal bill before a trip ends.
  • Fewer than 5% of expenses need correction or reversal once that capability exists.

14. Future enhancements

  • Payment links and local wallet/bank integration.
  • Settle-up actions and reminders.
  • Push/email invitations and activity updates.
  • Receipt attachments, edit/reversal flows, audit exports, multiple currencies, and richer roles.

15. MVP acceptance criteria

The MVP is ready for testing when a group can create and join a private trip, assign a cash holder, record THB contributions, record personal and shared-pool expenses, use all three split methods, see accurate group and personal totals, and understand each person’s due/credit position without outside calculations.
